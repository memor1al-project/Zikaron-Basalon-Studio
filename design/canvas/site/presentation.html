How it works

From your story to the evening, or a page of your own

1

Start from your story

Begin with everything gathered in the Collection Wizard: the photographs, letters and context already brought together.

2

Choose your output

A presentation script for the evening, a shareable memorial webpage, or something else. Tell the wizard what your evening needs.

3

AI drafts the narrative

Shaped around Zikaron BaSalon's own story arc (a hook, the world before, the heart of the story, a turning point, a closing message): a starting shape, not a finished script.

4

Review & refine

The host or family shapes the draft into their own voice: reordering, rewriting, adding what only they would know.

5

Final sign-off

Nothing is presented on the evening, or published as a webpage, without a person's final approval.

For after the story

Shape the future, together

These aren't political prompts, just a gentle way to sit with what was shared. The wizard offers these as closing cards, drawn from the same six starter-questions Zikaron BaSalon uses at the end of a real gathering.

How it works

From scattered pieces to one story

1

Upload & search materials

Photographs, letters, recordings, and personal items that carry a story: a passport, a postcard, a family heirloom. Search Zikaron BaSalon’s own archive alongside Yad Vashem, the Ghetto Fighters’ House, USC Shoah Foundation and USHMM, or find testimony from someone in the same town.

2

Discover connections

The AI suggests similar images and story threads, helping you see how separate fragments fit into one narrative. You decide what belongs.

3

Historical context, alongside your material

Relevant references and background surface next to what you’ve gathered, drawn automatically and cross-checked by the Zikaron BaSalon team.

4

Human review

Nothing moves forward until a family member or the Zikaron BaSalon team reviews it. The AI assists; people decide.
